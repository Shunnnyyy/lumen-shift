System logic

How the Observation Turns Into a Question

Input Photo + time + lux
Pattern Activity vs brightness
Question Does the light match use?

Record consistently

Use the same phone app, similar camera direction, and the same basic fields each time: location, time, lux, activity, and observation.

Compare patterns

A single lux reading is not the point. The value becomes useful when compared across streets, times, and activity levels.

Ask better questions

The field log helps identify places where the visible brightness feels higher than the observed activity.

Observation structure

What the Project Tracks

Photo

A visual record of the street, including glare, shadows, signs, windows, and visible movement.

Lux

A phone-based illuminance reading used for comparison, not scientific-grade precision.

Activity

A simple low, medium, or high activity label based on pedestrians, cars, bikes, and open businesses.

Pattern

A short note explaining whether the brightness seems aligned with actual street use.

Where the Ideas Come From

Outdoor lighting DarkSky lighting principles

Background reading on responsible outdoor lighting, glare, over-lighting, and the use of controls.

 Roadway lighting FHWA Lighting Handbook

A technical reference for roadway lighting concepts, visibility, dimming, and lighting design context.

 Adaptive systems Context-adaptive street lighting

Research on adaptive dimming, traffic-responsive lighting, long-term measurements, and system tradeoffs.

 Lighting basics DOE LED basics

Background reading on LED lighting, efficiency, and why lighting quality matters beyond brightness alone.

 Local context City of Toronto Open Data

A starting point for future neighborhood, infrastructure, and map-based public datasets.

 Night light data NASA Black Marble

Reference for satellite nighttime-light data and how artificial light can be studied at city scale.
